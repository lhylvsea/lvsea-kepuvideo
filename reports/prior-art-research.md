## Synthesis ledger

### keep

- Source-first facts and evidence ledger.
- Real audio/SRT as the timing source of truth.
- Staged preview and local deterministic rendering.
- Explicit technical, visual, rights and human-review evidence layers.

### adapt

- Replace the tutorial's humanizer-zh with the installed lvsea-writing contract.
- Preserve the article's practical stop points while adding explicit permissions, rollback and adjacent-skill exclusions.
- Generalize from 60 seconds to arbitrary durations and chaptered long-form knowledge videos.

### reject

- Directly copying other repositories or embedding their profile assets.
- Unreviewed remote installers, hidden paid TTS, voice cloning, public uploads and automatic social publishing.
- Claims that static validation proves output quality or human preference.

### invent

- A narrow trigger for lvsea-kepuvideo so it can coexist with lvsea-video.
- A deterministic project/SRT verifier and final media verifier.
- A writing adapter that makes lvsea-writing the final text-editing stage.
- A grilling gate for unresolved content, rights, cost, voice and visual decisions.

## Missing evidence

- No real knowledge-video project was rendered in this package-creation turn.
- No provider-backed TTS/HyperFrames output comparison or independent human blind review is available.
- SkillsMP did not provide a stable source artifact suitable for adoption in this run; it remains discovery-only evidence.
